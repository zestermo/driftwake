## About the game

You wake on the sand of Brinehollow, a sleepy fishing island at the edge of dangerous
waters. Make a captain, pick up a cutlass and earn your way off the island. Then take your
sloop out to sea and follow the lodestone down a chain of islands, past pirate fleets, the Admiralty,
reefs, whirlpools, storms and something very large in the deep.

Driftwake is inspired by shonen pirate adventures. Combat is fast and readable, and you build
a fighting style from your weapons, a web of skills and Will.

Nearly everything in the game is themed in a PSX style: the characters, islands, ships,
textures, music and sound effects. It's all rendered like a late-90s console game, with
wobbly vertices, dithered colour and a chunky pixel grid.

## Features

### Combat
- **Fighting styles set by what's in your hands:** cutlass, dual swords, katana (two-handed,
  with a charged iai draw), axe (a splitter combo, a whirlwind and the Skybreaker leap),
  pistol, dual pistols (gun kata and Gun Rain), and bare fists (a boxing stance).
- **Parry, block and dodge.** A well-timed parry staggers. Red "peril" attacks can't be
  blocked, only dodged, or broken with Iron Will.
- **Physics ragdolls, blood and dismemberment.** Heavy hits knock people flying, and a katana
  can cut a cannonball in half.
- **Skill trees (K):** a tree for every weapon, Base & Will and your Driftfruit. Weapon trees
  grow with mastery, and mastering a tree wakes its techniques' element. Put an ultimate on R.
- **Grapples and martial arts:** suplex, throw or giant-swing a pirate into his friends.
- **Enemies:** pirate grunts who circle, feint, shoot and raise the alarm; riflemen; scuttlebugs
  and canopy spitters; Captain Grell's den in the forest; the Brood Queen in her cave; Captain
  Morrow, a three-phase boss at Redtide Rock; and the Leviathan.

### Will
- **Iron Will and Weather Eye** awaken at level 10, Captain's Will at level 20.
- **Iron Will: Coat** turns your weapon arm and blade glossy black: nothing can block you, and
  every hit breaks red wind-ups.
- **Weather Eye** makes enemy wind-ups flash, widens your parry window, and with Foresight
  dodges the next hit for you.

### The sea
- **Wind and sailing:** sail where you like; a fair wind gives you extra speed. A braced yard,
  foam wakes and a swell that rolls the hull.
- **Naval fights:** enemy ships show their hull over the masthead; up close their guns won't
  bear, so they ram you or come alongside to board.
- **Ship damage:** cannon hits hole the hull, set fires and flood the ship. The crew patch,
  douse and pump to keep her afloat.
- **A sea you can hear and see:** surf on the shore, the open swell, the hull's wash and
  creaking timbers, gusting wind in the rigging; surf rolling over the shallows, whitecaps
  in a blow, wind streaks and spray over the bow.
- **The lodestone (hold H):** Captain Morrow's glass points to the next islands of the chain.
- **Hazards and plunder:** reefs, fog banks, whirlpools, storm cells, wrecks, bottles and buried
  treasure.
- **The sea chart (M)** fills in as you explore. Set your own marks to sail by.
- **Your ship:** climb the rigging to the crow's nest, swing from ropes off the yard. Below the
  quarterdeck is the crew cabin, with a bunk to rest in, a galley to restock provisions and the
  crew's shared storage chest. Ashore, hold B and she fades in and sails up to the nearest shore.
- **Death:** you wake in the cabin. Your bag waits on a grave where you fell. Die again before
  you get back to it and the grave sinks into the ground, with everything in it.

### Brinehollow
- **A story to start:** wake on the beach and follow Old Pell's lead to Captain Morrow, with
  your next goal on screen.
- **The island:** a harbour town with a stone quay, a village with a tavern, smithy and sea
  chapel, a lighthouse, jungle ruins, the smugglers' camp, a deep western forest and the
  brood cave.
- Traders who buy and sell: Nessa (treasure), Gus (food and drink), Marlo, Clothier Sela,
  Old Ida, Vey's Armoury, and Tackett the shipwright.
- **Item tiers:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Ultra and Supreme.
  Better tiers hit harder and guard better.
- 27 weapon designs, plus clothing and armour you can buy or find as plunder.

### Your captain
- A character creator with body, face, hair, hats and outfits. Hair, coats, capes and skirts
  move with physics.
- **Levels and skill trees,** with abilities that grow into new tiers the more you use them.

### World and co-op
- **A day/night cycle and weather:** sun and moon, clear skies, rain, storms with lightning,
  and 3D clouds you can fly through.
- **Co-op for up to four captains,** sharing one ship. Revive downed crewmates, and share
  loot and trades.
- **Saves:** three save slots with autosave.

The map (M) unrolls in your hands; scroll to zoom from the island out to the sea chart.

**At the helm:** W/S set the sail, A/D steer, left click fires a broadside, F leaves the wheel, and N lashes the helm.

**In the water:** Left Ctrl dives (hold to go deeper), Space surfaces, Shift for a burst. **Fishing:** with a rod in
the equipment slot, face the water and press F to cast, then F again when the bobber dips.

**Climbing ladders and rigging:** F grabs on, W/S climb, Space jumps off, and F lets go. With a
one-handed blade, Z draws it and left click chops while you hang on.

## Version history

Versions marked **unstable** are early builds for trying new things: newer, but less tested. The main download is
always the last stable version (v1.20); unstable ones are on [the releases page](https://driftwake.fun/releases/).

### v1.20.2: The Palace (October 24, 2026), unstable
The palace opens. The grounds' gate stands open and the road up the hill is a road you walk (its ends ramped, its
parapets keeping you on it), up to a new gate in the hilltop's ring wall, the court between the palace's wings, and
the throne hall: columns, a carpet to the dais, banners, chandeliers, Queen Ilsabet on her throne. Lord Chamberlain
Orme's three errands, each after the last: a petition sealed by the Guild, the Queen's stolen signet found on the
docks, and an audience with the Queen. People in raised rooms now stand on their floors.

### v1.20.1: The Docks and the Terrace (October 24, 2026), unstable
The first piece of 1.21, the cities. The royal capital opens beyond its core: a warehouse row east of the market
street leads to the docks' wharf and a postern onto the quay, with Marta Coyle's shipwright's yard (every refit
Tackett's yard does), Haskett's Chandlery (timber, canvas, iron) and Vesper's Corner, a fence who pays over the odds
for plunder. Under the palace wall, the noble quarter's garden terrace of cypresses and hedges, with Ashgrove &
Daughters, outfitters to the court (rare gear a tier above). Two new city quests: a foreman's lost manifest and a
lady's invitation carried to the Crown and Compass.

### v1.20: The World (October 24, 2026)
Every beast boss and roamer reworked: eight rebuilt bodies on a rig with jointed legs and bodies that follow the

that knocks you down. A boss ground for every island type: the jungle's canopy, an ice grotto on the snow, a

roofs and walls throughout, a campfire that looks like fire, and Driftwake's own names for its powers, places and

A pass over every island type. Pirate camps, watchtowers, gates and wells take each island's own roofs and walls

### v1.19.3: The Fog Isle (October 24, 2026), unstable
The fifth island type: a haunted isle under endless fog. Dead woods of bare oaks hung with grey moss, drowned

### v1.19.1: Beasts Reborn (October 23, 2026), unstable

### v1.19: Wanted (October 23, 2026)
Rival captains run by the game, built like yours: the same weapons, moves, skills and ultimates, reading your spacing,

the side at their own work (drilling, reading, tinkering, playing an instrument, sitting) or watching the fight.
They remember who won and come back stronger. A knockdown is never a loss: only being beaten is. A cutscene system
(letterboxed subtitles, voice blips, a co-op skip vote). Bounties for each captain with wanted posters, townsfolk who know your face and shops that change their prices; the Admiralty as grey
ironclad patrols, bounty hunters ashore and three named officers with squads. Lose to the Admiralty and break out of
their brig. A new opening: a narrated storm at sea and a fight on Captain Maren Holt's deck. Fighting in the water
with your own weapon, and spear, hammer, greatsword, twin-axe and black-leg pirates on the chain islands.

Five new weapon kinds, each with its own stance, moves, three-branch tree, element and ultimate: polearms (spear,

### v1.18.2: Every Wake a Lesson (October 20, 2026)
Skills rebuilt from the ground up. Each weapon has its own tree in three branches that grows only while you fight

### v1.14: Smooth Sailing (October 15, 2026)
The performance update: the last few releases had slowed Brinehollow to 20-30 fps; it now runs about 4.5x faster.
- **Far actors sleep:** pirates, bugs, spitters, enemy ships and villagers past ~160 m from every captain stop
  thinking, moving and animating, and wake within ~120 m where they were (villagers back on their routine); co-op aware.
- **Fewer draws:** a body is ~15 surfaces instead of 23, kit pieces get visibility ranges by size, small things stop
  casting shadows, sun shadows reach 80 m, fog banks are one MultiMesh each.
- **Calmer physics:** hurtboxes and interactables no longer monitor (6 of 720 areas do), moored hulls rest far off,
  at most 4 physics steps a frame; Weather, birds, clouds, coast checks, spray and puddles update less often.
- **Measured** (dev laptop, Brinehollow route): 42.3 -> 9.2 ms a frame at midday (~24 -> ~108 fps), 35.9 -> 9.3 at
  night, 32.1 -> 10.1 in rain. The F8 overlay shows enemies awake / asleep.
- **Also:** stance and parry polish (twin daggers, one dagger, dual swords, with parry flourishes), arms no longer
  stretch after knockdowns or hand-placed weapons and instruments, combo/reload/input-buffer timing in game time,
  nothing kills a duelist mid-duel.

## Running from source

1. Install [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/download) (standard build, not .NET).
2. Clone this repository and open `project.godot` in Godot.
3. Press **F5** to play. The game opens on the title screen.

To play co-op, one captain picks **Co-op → Host** on the title screen and the others
**Join** with the host's IP address (port 24680).
