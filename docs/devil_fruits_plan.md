# Devil Fruits: the design for their return (agreed with Zach, 2026-10-15)

Fruits come back before 2.0 (docs/roadmap_v2.md, phase 3), polished to the weapons' standard (own moves, effects and
tree). Today they're out of the world (`DevilFruits.IN_WORLD` false since 2026-10-09); the Ember, Wolf (Zoan) and Vine
fruits and the fruit tab on the skill map exist. This doc settles two questions first: why not eat one, and how a
crew saves a fruit user from the sea.

## Why not eat a fruit (or the first one you find)

### Waiting is a real choice
- **One fruit per captain, forever.** Eating a second one kills you (as in the source).
- **Tiers:** joke fruits (niche, funny powers), solid fruits, and rare/legendary ones. The first fruit you find is
  probably not the one you want.
- **An uneaten fruit is worth a fortune:** sell it to a rare-gear merchant for a huge sum, trade it to a crewmate,
  or hand it over for quests that want one (a collector, a cult). Eating it gives all that up.

### The sea has value a fruit user loses
- **Diving for treasure:** loot on the seabed, in wrecks and sunken ruins, pearls, sea chests. A fruit user can't
  fetch it themselves.
- **The Seafarer tree** (only for captains without a fruit): faster swimming, longer breath, fighting underwater, a
  dive-attack onto boarding ships, faster climbing aboard, stamina from spray in a storm. "No fruit" is a build, not a
  gap.
- **Haki leans toward non-users:** anyone can learn it, but captains without a fruit progress faster or reach the top
  tier sooner, so a pure swordsman/Haki build stands beside fruit builds.

### The weakness bites
- **Seawater:** fall overboard and you sink, you don't swim.
- **Standing water:** up to the waist slows you and blocks your powers; deeper and you start sinking. A storm washing
  over the deck weakens you.
- **Enemies exploit it:** pirates shove fruit users toward the rail, sea monsters drag them under, and the Marines
  (phase 6) bring seastone nets and cuffs that cancel powers.

## Saving a fruit user (co-op)
1. A fruit user who falls in goes limp and sinks slowly, with a breath meter (about 25-30 s).
2. Every crewmate gets an alert ("<name> is sinking!") with a marker and the depth.
3. A crewmate without a fruit dives (Ctrl), grabs them (F) and swims them up; slower while carrying.
4. At the surface, the rescuer tows them to a ladder or beach and pushes them up; the rescued captain is weakened for
   a moment.
5. From the ship: a thrown life ring, or a rope-and-hook from the rail, hauls a sinking crewmate up (so fruit users
   can save each other).
- Host-arbitrated like the other co-op grabs (driftwake-coop); works for guests.

## Solo
- Sinking counts as a normal death (you wake in the cabin). Your bag doesn't sink out of reach: it **washes up on the
  nearest shore**.
- A buyable **life buoy** quick item saves you once.
