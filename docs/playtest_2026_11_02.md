## Fix plan

**A. Bugs with a clear fix (one fixer pass each, atomic tests):**
- 5 the vault: move the chest off the dais and mark it on the map; fix the two ids; null-guard `ItemDB.tiered`.
  Test that the chest is reachable.
- 11, 10 the drills accept Iai/Technique; no mastery or points while on the rack; keeping a weapon finds its tree; the
  cutlass is no longer found at start; a "talk to Vey" step between the rack and the drills.
- 7 the rush lane stops at water or a drop, a hard skid at the end; Morrow's lane clamped inside his wall.
- 3 FLEE counts as pursuing; an ~8 s grace on the island and smoke checks; a floor on the weather lose range.
- 2 the chase timer runs only while hunting; a distance-based edge; pirates get it too.
- 8 hp_scale 1+0.85(n-1); poise scales; stronghold bosses scale.
- 1 the capital skip uses `capital`/`city`/`stronghold`; the test built from real chain nodes; the warning reworded.
- The log errors: `beam` → dark, `stone`/`cloth` → existing textures.

**B. Bigger but clear:**
- 4 the Admiralty spawns through the worker prebuild, one ship a frame (host and guests).
- 6 the capital's back houses keep box colliders; the fortress districts get floor boxes; ray-down tests over every
  roof and floor; an "Unstuck" in the pause menu, plus an automatic warp below the terrain.

**C. Design, Zach's call first (driftwake-grill or a mock):**
- 12 guidance after the story: a standing chain objective (sail to the next island, its arc, its boss); the capital
  lines and the stronghold as journal quests; how notifications and the map tie in.
- 1 a rival crew at sea (a rival hull you board), or the shore visit made obvious.
- 9 a beast "clearing" move and knockback that slides along walls.
- 7 the Redtide massif: a natural mountain ring round the arena.
- 5 Driftfruit in the world: where it drops (the vault, the arena champion, the deep) now it's expected.
