# Rival pirate crews (Zach, 2026-10-19): the headline of v1.19

## What it is
A rival pirate crew lands on the island you're exploring. A short back-and-forth cutscene with dynamic camera moves and
dialogue sets up the standoff. Then the fight breaks out the way One Piece crews fight: matched pairs. Their gunner
goes after the captain with the highest gun skill, their best swordsman after your best swordsman, and the captains
always face each other. Rivals fight like other players would: the same weapons, skills, techniques and ultimates
as the captains, moving and pressing, with fun lines thrown at you and your crew mid-fight. Beat them badly enough
and their captain calls the retreat: they run to their ship and sail off the island. Every crew you meet can come
back a few islands later, a little stronger, matched to your level, with new weapons and abilities, and they
remember the last fight.

## Decided (2026-10-19)

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Release | **The headline of 1.19**, after the 1.18 batch ships. | 1.18 is already full. Rivals deserve their own release. | — |
| Matchups (co-op) | Pairs by specialty: their gun fighter takes the captain with the highest gun mastery, their best blade takes your best blade, the captains always face each other. Extra rivals and extra players pair off by mastery. | The One Piece crew-vs-crew fight. | After play |
| Solo | **Duels in turn.** Their crew comes at you one at a time, the best match for your strongest weapon first, while the rest circle and taunt. The captain comes last. Each duel is a real one-on-one and a moving fight across the terrain. | Keeps the matched-duel feel with one captain. | After play |
| How rivals fight | The **same weapons, skills, techniques and ultimates as the players**, used by an AI that moves and pressures like a player would (spacing, dodges, combos, ultimates at the right moment), not a grunt's attack loop. | "Fought by seemingly other players." | — |
| Voice | **Speech bubbles plus generated voice blips**, pitch and timbre per rival. Lines at the cutscene, during the fight (taunts, reactions to hits, ultimates, a downed crewmate), at the retreat. No recorded voice acting. | The audio is all generated. Readable and punchy. | — |
| The intro | A short **cutscene**: rivals land or walk up, a back-and-forth with camera cuts and moves, then the fight starts. Skippable. In co-op every screen sees it. | The shonen standoff. | — |
| Winning | Beat them badly enough and **their captain calls a retreat**: they run to their ship and sail away. Rewards follow the economy (gold, a chance at a trait weapon, renown later with bounties). | A rival who escapes can come back. | Economy pass |
| Losing | **They plunder and gloat**: their captain takes a share of your gold (or a treasure), gloats, and they sail off. You're downed but alive. The grudge shows in their lines next time. | Losing to a rival should sting and set up a rematch. | — |
| Recurring | Each crew is **persistent**: name, captain, members, looks, weapons, voice. It comes back after a few islands, scaled to the crew's level, with new weapons and abilities, and remembers wins and losses. | Rivals you get to know. | — |

### Open / deferred

- Crew size and how many rival crews exist per save: **assumed** 3-5 members, a few crews per chain, from seeded pools.
- The cutscene tool (camera rails/shots, dialogue staging): **assumed** a small reusable cutscene system that 1.19's
  rivals build first, which later story scenes reuse.
