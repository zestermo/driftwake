Postcards from the chain

What's out there

Islands nobody's seen

Past Redtide Rock, every island is built from your world's seed: its coast, its jungle, its town, its secrets. Your friend's chain won't match yours. Compare notes anyway.

Towns with a pulse

Stilt villages, walled trading posts, sleepy hamlets, whole towns. The locals work, break for lunch, drink at the inn and go to bed. Some have gossip. Some have jobs. Some have both.

Harbours with character

Tie up at a stilt port, a busy wharf, a crumbling old pier or a smugglers' cove with a red lamp and a strict no-questions policy.

Down in the dark

Bug tunnels, a smugglers' grotto, drowned shrines, bat roosts and a ruined temple. Each one ends in a boss and a chest. Usually in that order.

Big things in the trees

The Silverback fights from the canopy, and it has company: the Strangler, the Shade and the Mantis Queen. Alpha boars and a grazing Thornback roam the rest.

Fight like a shonen hero

Cutlass, katana, axe, twin blades, sword and pistol, and daggers. Combos, techniques, an ultimate for every weapon. Parry, dodge, and break red wind-ups with Haki.

Your ship, your problem

Trim the sail, run out the guns, board a brig and take her prize. When she's holed, patch, pump and douse before she goes down. Then argue about whose fault it was.

Up to four captains

One hosts, friends join by address, and you all share one ship. Revive each other when it goes wrong. It will go wrong.

All of it made in code

Bodies, islands, ships, textures, music and sound, drawn the way a PS1 would: wobbly vertices, dithered colour, a chunky pixel grid. Very few hand-made assets were harmed.

Download for WindowsLatest release · .zip

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Every version and what changed.

Won't open? The game isn't code-signed yet, so Windows blocks it quietly the first time. Two quick steps:

  1. Unzip the download first (right-click the zip, Extract All). Don't run it from inside the zip.
  2. Right-click Driftwake.exe, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom, then OK. Double-click and set sail.

If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box shows up instead, click More info, then Run anyway.
