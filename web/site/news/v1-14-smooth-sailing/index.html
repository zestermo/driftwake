v1.14: Smooth Sailing

The game had got slow. Now it isn't.

The last few updates piled a lot onto the islands: villages full of people with routines, harbours, caves, jungle bosses, lanterns and lit windows, a living sea with spray and birds, instruments and duels. I love every one of them. But stacked together, they dragged the game down. On a solid gaming PC (a Ryzen 5 5600G with an RTX 3080) Brinehollow was running at 20 to 30 frames per second, and honestly, it had stopped being fun to play. So v1.14 is all about putting that right.

What was going wrong

The graphics card was barely breaking a sweat. The processor was the one drowning. When you stood on Brinehollow's beach, the world held about 200 enemies, but only around 35 of them were anywhere near you. The rest were on the islands ahead, two kilometres away, and every one of them was still thinking, walking and animating every single frame as if you were standing right next to them. On top of that, every slow frame made the physics run extra catch-up steps, which made the next frame slower still.

What we did

  • Far-off things take a nap. Pirates, bugs, enemy ships and villagers more than about 160 m from every captain now sleep: no thinking, no moving, no animating. Get within about 120 m and they wake up exactly where they were, and villagers pick their routine back up. It works the same for your whole crew in co-op.
  • Less to draw. Each character is drawn in about 15 pieces instead of 23, small props fade out at a distance and stop casting shadows, and repeated things like fog banks are drawn as one. Draw calls in Brinehollow dropped by about a third.
  • Calmer physics. Hundreds of hitboxes and interaction spots stopped constantly checking for overlaps (only 6 of 720 still do), moored ships rest until you come near, and a slow frame can no longer snowball into more and more physics catch-up.
  • Smarter background work. The weather, birds, clouds, shoreline sounds, spray and rain puddles update a little less often, and the inventory screen stopped quietly running in the background the whole game.

The numbers

Measured on the same laptop along the same route through Brinehollow (the beach, the plaza, the market, the quay, the dock, the den, the training yard and aboard the ship), averaged across the whole route:

Brinehollowv1.13v1.14
Midday, clear~24 fps~108 fps
Night~28 fps~107 fps
Midday, rain~31 fps~99 fps

That's roughly four and a half times faster, and the town looks exactly the same as it did.

What you'll notice

  • Towns, the market and fights feel smooth, with far fewer hitches, and slower PCs gain the most.
  • Distant camps sit quiet until you get closer, then spring to life. They were always there; they're just not burning your processor while you're on the other side of the island.
  • Press F8 for the performance overlay. It now shows how many enemies are awake and how many are asleep.

A few more things in this one

  • Stances and parries. Twin daggers sit lower and wider, a single dagger's off hand hangs low at your side, and dual swords settle lower. Dual swords, one dagger and twin daggers each get their own block, parry and a new flourish when a parry lands.
  • No more stretchy captains. Getting knocked down or holding certain weapons and instruments could slowly stretch your arms until you re-equipped something. Fixed.
  • Tighter timing. Combos, reloads and buffered inputs now count in game time, so a hitch can no longer throw them off, and nothing can actually kill you mid-duel any more.

Thanks for sticking with it while things got sluggish. Every change is in the release notes, and what's next is on the roadmap.
