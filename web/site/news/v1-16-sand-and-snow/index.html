v1.16: Sand and Snow

The chain isn't all jungle any more.

Ever since the island chain opened up, every island past Brinehollow has been some kind of jungle. Different layouts, different villages, different beasts, but always palms and vines and the same green canopy. v1.16 changes that. The chain now rolls three kinds of island, and two of them are brand new: islands buried in snow, and islands baking under a desert sun. Each one has its own land, towns, people, wildlife, bosses, weather and music.

Snow islands

A snow island is built around a tall peak with pointed spires, frozen lakes in its hollows and a frozen river running down to the sea. The villages are round pink-brick houses under tiered green roofs, with snowy fences round the yards, iron lamps and a tame shaggy beast standing about by the market. Most islands have a castle on the summit. Look out for ice-block ruins, a town buried in the drifts, a ship frozen into the pack ice just off the beach, and caves full of icicles and glowing crystals with a frozen waterfall at the end.

The weather is its own thing up there: a white haze nearly all the time, a light snowfall that never quite stops, and every so often heavy snow or a full blizzard that closes the world in to a few metres. Your breath mists in the cold.

The wildlife bites back. Wolves hunt in packs, circling and lunging one at a time while the lead howls. White bears rear and charge, walrus bulls guard their colonies, hares bolt in zigzags, and herds of huge shagbacks graze quietly until you hit one and the whole herd stampedes. The bosses are the White Shade, the Bear King and the Ice Golem, with Frostfang at the ruins and the Rimeback, a mammoth with ice on its back, roaming the island.

Desert islands

The desert islands are the biggest in the game: plateaus and mesas, rock ridges, dune fields, dry riverbeds, canyons, salt pans and oases with palms leaning over the water. Towns are flat-roofed houses in pastel washes with domes, awnings and rooftop terraces, and the biggest ones are packed medinas with minarets and a great domed hall. You can walk into the houses, the caravanserai and the potter's workshop.

Out in the sand you'll find caravan camps, an obelisk, two giant seated guardians, a ruined skyline with a great dome torn open, a city buried up to its windows, and a tomb cut into a rose sandstone cliff with a treasure inside. It never rains here. Instead the wind picks up and a sandstorm rolls in, the sky turns ochre and the lamps come on.

Scorpions, monitor lizards and fennec foxes live out in the open, and vultures circle overhead. The bosses are the Sand King (a sandworm that hunts you from under the sand), the Dune Emperor (a giant scorpion), the Basilisk and the Sand Lion, plus a hyena alpha, the Dune Strider roaming the dunes, and a bandit chief with a scimitar running the pirates' camp.

Footprints

Walk through snow or sand and you leave a trail of boot prints that slowly fills back in. In snow your boots sink into a soft layer and kick up little puffs; in sand you leave a darker trail. It works for your whole crew in co-op, and every step sounds like what you're standing on, ice included.

A few more things in this one

  • New music. Eight new tracks: the desert and snow wilds and their towns, each with a night version, plus wind, sandstorm and blizzard ambience.
  • Smoke, dust and fire. A whole library of hand-drawn sprite effects now drives the smoke, dust, sand, snow, mist and blood across the game, and fire and explosions throw a brief flash of light.
  • Kraken's Wake, redrawn. The cutlass's ultimate now pours out flowing, ink-drawn water: coils winding round you, arcs through every cut, and curling waves rising under the enemies you tear through. With Call of the Tide learned, water winds round your drawn cutlass too.
  • Capes and the cloak. Capes are a tattered trapezoid with painted folds and stay off your legs, Sela sells a heavy cloak, and the belt lantern hangs on your hip instead of your back.

Every change is in the release notes, and what's next is on the roadmap. Wrap up warm, bring water.
