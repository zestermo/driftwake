v1.17: Timber and Tide

The woods turn gold, and your ship grows up.

v1.17 does three big things. The island chain gets its fourth kind of island, a forest in full autumn colour. Your sloop stops being a fixed boat and becomes something you build up, level by level, with what you take from the ships you sink. And Brinehollow, the town every voyage starts from, grows into a proper port.

Forest islands

A forest island is rolling hills of maple, oak, beech and birch in reds, oranges and golds, with leaves piled on the ground and firs standing dark among them. Rivers run downhill to the sea, and the ones with falls drop into a gorge with a pool at the foot. The town always stands on a river: log cabins and timber-framed houses under steep shingle roofs, a sawmill with a turning water wheel and a log flume, and out in the woods charcoal burners' camps and a hunter's lodge.

Each island rolls one of four woodlands (autumn maple, birch and pine, an ancient oak grove, misty firs) and its own set of places: a waterfall gorge with a cave beside the curtain and rope bridges across, a ruined castle with outlaws in the courtyard and a keep to climb, and a hollow giant tree with a fungus stair up the outside and a root cavern behind it.

The woods are old and not everything in them is an animal. Red deer graze in herds until you hit one and the stag charges, foxes slip through the bracken, will-o'-wisps drift away ahead of you at night, wood sprites wait under the leaves, and some of those stumps walk. The bosses are the Stag King and the Elder Treant, with a moss golem, the Cursed Huntsman and his hounds running the outlaws' camp, and the Wild Hunt riding the island after dark. The forest has its own music, day and night, and a woodland ambience of wind, birdsong, woodpeckers and owls.

Build up your ship

Tackett's yard used to sell four one-time refits. Now it sells twelve upgrade lines, each with several levels, and every level shows on the sloop: hull plating, the gun deck, sails and rigging, rudder and helm, a copper bottom, a bow chaser, a crow's nest lookout, lanterns, railings, a tangerine grove you can pick for a heal, galley and bunks, and a bigger hold. The higher levels need salvage: timber, iron, canvas and copper, floating from the ships you sink (the bigger the ship, the better the haul) and stashed in wrecks and chests. In co-op anyone can chip in. If you bought refits before, they carry over as levels.

There's a new skill tree to go with it. Seamanship is each captain's own, earned by sailing, gunnery, repairs and working the pump, and its bonuses apply at the station you're manning, so a crew that splits up between the helm, the guns and the deck jobs sails better than four captains doing the same thing.

Brinehollow grows up

The island is half again as big, growing south into farmland around a windmill hamlet, with a waterfall pool to swim in, a wreck on the rocks and a second cove with a fisher's hut. The town itself has a market street of shopfronts climbing from the quay, a second row of houses on the hill behind it, and a shrine terrace with a pagoda at the top of a long stair. Woods now wrap the whole town and climb the hills behind it.

It's livelier too. Shopkeepers keep their counters by day and drink at the Salted Gull after dark, paper lanterns hang across the market street, chimneys smoke, and fishing boats work the harbour while dockers load the merchantman. Every building has a proper door and steps you can walk up. And a few shops moved: Haldor's Forge sells the weapons now, Corin has a music shop on the market street, and Vey sticks to armour.

A few more things in this one

  • The katana, redrawn. Its three cuts, the air slash and all five skills follow the blade's real path, with drawn steel slashes that match the swing. Awakened, they billow into steel-blue cloud and sakura petals, and petals drift off the blade.
  • Co-op clocks. A guest's clock now settles right after joining, so the waves don't run out of step with the host's for the first few seconds.

Every change is in the release notes, and what's next is on the roadmap. Mind the woods after dark.
