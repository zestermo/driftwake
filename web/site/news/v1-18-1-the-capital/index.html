v1.18.1: A First Look at the Capital

Two islands out from Brinehollow, the chain's first real city.

Cities were planned for later, but I couldn't wait to start on one, so here's a small update with a sneak peek. Sail two islands down the chain from Brinehollow and the next stop is no longer an ordinary island: it's the First Sea's royal capital. Every world rolls its own name for it: Port Solenne, Highcrown or Meridian.

Sailing in

You see it from a long way out: a sea wall of white stone and terracotta towers, a lighthouse and a fort on the harbour moles, and a palace on a hill above it all. Tie up at the quay, walk in through the sea gate and climb the market street, with the palace at the top of the view the whole way up.

What's in this first piece

  • The market and its shops. A plaza with a fountain and market stalls, and the capital's traders, including a curio dealer whose rare stock beats anything on the islands.
  • The Crown and Compass. An inn to rest and save at, a notice board, and townsfolk with their own things to say and a few city quests for the journal.
  • The palace and the arena. Seen from the street, guarded and closed for now. You'll get in later.
  • Gallows Row. Every capital has a part of town the guards don't walk. This one has a yard of toughs who don't like visitors.
  • People. The busiest streets in the game, built to stay smooth, and the windows light up after dark.

The rest of the city (the palace itself, the arena, the side streets and districts behind the fronts) comes with the full cities update in v1.20, along with a second city. This is the first slice, so let me know how it feels to arrive somewhere big. It works in co-op too: the whole crew gets the same city, the same stock and the same crowd.

The full list is in the release notes.
