v1.21: Cities

Two cities to get lost in, a second ship to sail, and an arena for anyone who wants a fair fight.

The capital was a harbour, a market street and a lot of walls you couldn't go behind. Now it's a city. There's a second one down the chain, built on the water, and it builds ships. This release came out in pieces over the last two days as unstable builds; v1.21 is all of them together.

The capital, opened up

Past the market street there's a warehouse row down to the docks, where Marta Coyle runs a shipwright's yard, and a chandler and a fence who pays over the odds for plunder. Under the palace wall there's a garden terrace and an outfitter to the court. The palace itself is open: walk the road up the hill, through the gate into its court and into the throne hall, where Queen Ilsabet holds court. Her Lord Chamberlain has errands for a captain who can keep a secret.

The arena

The Grand Arena fills up for anyone willing to climb its ladder: six named fighters, a rookie at the bottom and the Champion at the top, each fighting like a captain with their own weapon and skills. Nobody dies on the sand. Fall under a tenth of your health and the bout's over. Every win pays a purse. Beat the Champion for the title and the Champion's Sabre, then defend it against a challenger once a day. The blades are wooden, for appearances.

The canal city

A lagoon city of shipbuilders: houses crowded along canals and little squares, bridges over the water, and boatmen who'll row you between landings. There's a market by the Grand Canal, a glassworks, an inn, a church on the piazza and an arsenal where they build ships on the slips.

The caravel

Master Ottavio Grane at the arsenal will build you a caravel: faster and quicker to turn than the sloop, a bow chaser and a bigger hold, but a lighter hull and no guns on the quarterdeck. It's a different ship, not a better one. Your sloop isn't sold: she's laid up with her upgrades and paint, and any city shipwright will swap you between them.

Out on the chain

About half the harbours down the chain now have someone by the pier: a caulker, a gunsmith, a sailmaker, a joiner or a carpenter, each doing their part of a shipwright's work, or a chandler selling what a yard needs.

Lived-in streets, and a new sky

Both cities got a pass to make them feel lived in. Smoke from the chimneys, pigeons that scatter when you walk through them, lanterns by the doors, ivy and flower pots, more people in the lanes. Overhead there's a new sky of rolling clouds that build and drift and catch the light at dawn and dusk. Dive by day and the sun comes down through the water in shafts.

What's next

Crews and parties. Up to five captains in a crew, a party you can see at a glance, quests the whole crew shares, and better maps and quest markers: v1.22, next on the roadmap. Combat and gear moves to v1.23, strongholds and Driftfruits to v1.24.

Every change is in the release notes. Mind the gondolas.
