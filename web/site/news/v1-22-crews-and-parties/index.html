v1.22: Crews and Parties

Bring four friends. Name your crew, paint its mark on the sail, and split the loot.

Co-op has been there since the early builds, but it was always a few captains sharing a world. This release makes them a crew: one with a name, a flag, a roster, and a way to find each other again.

Crews of five

Up to five captains sail together now. The crew is saved in the Captain's world: its name, its flag, and everyone who's ever sailed with it. Open Crew in the pause menu to see who's aboard (and their ping), who's sailed with you before and when, and, if you're the Captain, to rename the crew, change the flag, or send someone ashore. Crews you've joined show up on the title screen's co-op panel, so getting back aboard is one click.

Your mark on the sail

The crew's emblem is painted on the main sail now, and it billows and furls with the canvas. There are six new emblems to choose from: a kraken, a trident, a rose, a sun, a wave and a raven. Tackett's yard in Brinehollow does the basics; the finer refits, like copper bottoms, bow chasers and the crow's nest, are first seen at a city's yard.

Sail as one

A quest pays the whole crew, wherever they are, so splitting up to cover an island doesn't cost anyone their share. The party panel tells you where your crewmates are and what they're doing: who's down, how far and which way, who's at the helm or on the guns, who's in the water.

Loot worth finding

Enemies don't shower you in gear any more. A grunt drops his weapon rarely, beasts give up a tusk or a hide now and then, and chests are where the good things are. Every chest is rolled for each captain on their own, around the level of the island it's on, so two crewmates opening the same chest find different things. That goes for Brinehollow's chests too: no more identical blue pistol for everyone. Purple and gold gear waits on the later islands and with the traders who stock it.

The map

Hold middle mouse to pan the map. Your crewmates are named on the chart (a downed one in red), the Captain's marks are drawn bigger, and there's a legend. On screen, only the four nearest marks show, with a count of the rest, so a crew of five doesn't bury the view in flags.

A few more things in this one

  • Waking on the beach. The opening at sea is set aside for now: a new game starts on Brinehollow's beach.
  • Lighter far crowds. Far-off townsfolk, flocks and foam move on the graphics card, a little faster everywhere.

What's next

Combat and gear: a pass over every weapon, new weapon kinds, and armour and clothes with stats. v1.23, next on the roadmap.
