v1.23: Bounties and Beasts

The playtest pass: the things that kept a long co-op session from being great.

We played a long co-op session on 1.22 and wrote down everything that got in the way. This release is that list, worked through.

Poise and juggles

Knockdowns are earned now. Every hit wears down a foe's poise, and when it breaks they stagger or hit the ground. Launchers throw them up and the air step chases them, so a good string turns into a juggle. Grunts block about a quarter of the blows you aim at their front instead of holding guard forever, and black leg has new moves.

Beasts that escalate

The Silverback, the Sand King and the Bear King fight in three phases, each with new moves and a moment when they change. Break their parts for trophies and weaker attacks. Roaming beasts have a temper: some posture, some stalk, some bolt, and if you back off, some settle down.

Wanted

Your poster and your bounty log are in the menu. Heat brings hunters and rival crews, and it cools over a few days. Enemy ships chase and ram, the Admiralty blockades and follows your wake, and Marine outposts guard some islands: raid them for what's inside, or sneak past.

Villages and the ship

Villages have tailors, smiths, armourers, luthiers and apothecaries, and a ! or ? over anyone with a quest. The tea house's master teaches black leg. Meals go on the galley table, ship storage has personal and shared tabs, and you can rest and respec at any bunk or inn.

Story and help

Cutscenes wait for you and let you reply. Vey's drills in the yard teach the controls, and each menu explains itself the first time you open it. You can drop quests and choose which one to track.

Fixes

  • No more falling under the island.
  • Bosses don't wander out of their arena and heal.
  • Cleared quests stay cleared.

What's next

Combat and gear: crew techniques, new Will paths, armour with stats and ranged weapons. v1.24, next on the roadmap.

Every change is in the release notes.
