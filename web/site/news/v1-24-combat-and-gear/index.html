v1.24: Combat and Gear

Fight as a crew, wear gear that matters, and bring a cannon.

This one is about the fight itself: what you do with your crewmates, what you wear into it, and what you fire.

Crew techniques

In combat stance, press C to do a two-person move for your weapon with the nearest crewmate: launches, throws, covering fire, a shield wall, a crewmate riding your cannonball. Sailing alone? A solo version stands in, and for some moves your ship does.

Four Will paths

The Captain's Will has four new paths, each with upgrades. Tempo and Fury change the rhythm of a fight. Tide lets you run across deep water and raise a swell. Bond ties you to a crewmate: a shared gauge, a Twin Finisher, and Shelter when one of you is in trouble.

Gear with stats

Every piece of armour and clothing has weight and stats, scaled by rarity, and matching pieces add up to set bonuses. Heavy gear blocks more and slows you down; a full gold heavy kit takes about half the damage off a hit.

Ranged weapons

Three new weapon kinds, each with its own tree:

  • Long guns. Muskets, rifles, a blunderbuss and more. Tap to fire, or hold to steady a shot that hits harder and goes through the first foe.
  • Cannons. A ball that bursts where it lands and knocks everyone down, a hard kick back, and a long reload. Fire one at your feet in the air and it throws you up.
  • Slingshots. Stones as fast as you can click, a power draw, and trick pellets: fire, smoke, blast and sticky.

Find them in chests and at the smiths; cannons are rarer.

Cloth that behaves

Capes, coats, skirts and hair move in a new, faster cloth solver that keeps them off the body. Coat tails hang the way they're cut instead of flaring out, skirts and tunics under a long coat stop poking through it, and vests fit at the shoulders.

What's next

Strongholds and Driftfruits: raids on Admiralty strongholds and pirate hideouts, and the fruits back, done right. v1.25, next on the roadmap.

Every change is in the release notes.
