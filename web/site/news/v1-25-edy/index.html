v1.25: Edy

Find out what kind of Edy runs in you, and watch it burn.

The Will paths have always had a force behind them without a name. Now it has one: Edy, the way water turns back on itself behind a rock, round and round, never still and never gone.

What kind are you?

Climb the shrine stair to Hana Teal's teahouse and she'll read your Edy with five questions. There are five kinds: Still, Ember, Crown, Storm and Tide. Each has its own colour and its own aura, and each leans you a little toward one way of fighting: wider dodge and parry windows, heavier heavies, a quicker ultimate, a burst of speed after a dodge, or getting your crew back on their feet faster. Read wrong? There's a reader on the capital's terrace. She charges like a duchess.

Infuse and Reinforce

Coat is now Infuse: your Edy pours down your forearms and into the blade, flames licking off your hands. Hardening is now Reinforce, a skill that wraps your whole body for fifteen seconds: quicker, lighter on stamina, harder to hurt, and light blows don't rock you. Both hit with a wind blast when you power up.

Auras when it matters

No glowing all the time. The aura shows when you use your Edy: a tall flame round you, a glowing outline, a circle on the ground in your type's shape that stays where you stood when you jump. Big Edy moves flare for a moment. There's a hum while it burns, a whoosh when it flares, and a soft pulse with a red glint in your eyes when you sense a blow and slip it.

One Captain page

The Captain page is one page now: the seven Edy paths across the top, the one you're looking at as a ladder, your level perks in a popup. Gold pips show you anything waiting to be spent, from the menu tab down to the exact card. Menus are crisper on bigger screens, new loot is tagged NEW, gear that beats what you're wearing gets a green arrow, vendors wear a sign of their trade, and each weapon tree tells you its crew technique.

More to wear, play and look like

  • The teahouse. Bao the cook teaches black leg out front now, and his pupil Tomo waits for you on the flat, bows, and counts you in.
  • Music. The banjo, at Corin's, and a new capstan shanty for the whole band: "Roll the Wake Down".
  • Gear. The Bulwark set (helm, brigandine, gauntlets: cheaper blocks, stamina back on a parry), the Bosun's Coat, a Powder Belt and a Shark's Tooth.
  • Weapons. The Sharkfin Cutlass, the Marlinspike, the Whaler's Harpoon and the Musketoon.
  • Looks. Two new faces, glare and cat eyes, dreads and a mohawk, hair dyes and two-tone or tipped finishes.

What's next

Strongholds and Driftfruits: raids on Admiralty strongholds and pirate hideouts, and the fruits back, done right. v1.26, next on the roadmap.
