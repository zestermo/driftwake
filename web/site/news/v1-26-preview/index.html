Coming in 1.26

We played for two hours and wrote down everything. Here's what we're doing about it.

After Edy shipped, we sailed a crew of two from Brinehollow to level thirteen, island after island, and kept a list. Some of it was bugs. A lot of it was the game not being as fun as it should be yet: fights that kept ending in ragdolls, an Admiralty that never caught you but never left you alone, villages that felt copy-pasted. 1.26 is that list, worked through.

Already in the test builds

We're shipping 1.26 in pieces as unstable builds, 1.25.1, 1.25.2 and on, so you can play the fixes as they land. Turn on Get unstable updates in Options and the title screen offers each one as a small patch. The releases page has them too.

  • Fair fights. You have poise now, like your enemies: one heavy blow staggers you, the second puts you down, then a moment where nothing can. Grunts take more before they fall, knockback is softer, and every creature glows red as it winds up. Chains finally chain.
  • The Admiralty, tuned. Calmer heat, a quiet spell after every chase, cutters that run you down with a bow gun instead of turning broadside and losing you, and a three-ship blockade you break by sinking any one of them.
  • Visitors who knock first. Rival crews, officers and bounty hunters are announced, then land and walk in: slower, tougher, better fights.
  • A compass and a flask. The compass stays up on foot with your crewmates and your objective on it. Every captain carries a tonic flask: three draughts, refilled when you rest.
  • And a lot of small ones. Boss XP for the whole crew, XP from quests, pirates back on your route, traders who stay at their stalls, villagers who don't spin round every time you walk past.

What we're building now

  • The Edy tree. The paths become one round tree of eight branches round your Edy, with bridges between them. Abilities rank up as you use them: Infuse hardens, then overflows; Foresight starts dodging blows for you, then sees them coming. A new branch, Infusion, pours your Edy's look into every technique, and then every blow.
  • A new opening. You wake on Brinehollow with nothing. Fight with your fists first, then pick your weapon from Sergeant Vey's rack, any of them, after trying them all. Learn your Edy from the master at the shrine at the top of the long stair. The story takes you right across the island.
  • Villages worth finding. Shops you walk into, and somewhere special in every village: a library, a hot spring, a legendary swordsmith. Gossip that leads somewhere, tavern games, and island stories where the side jobs matter: every bandit you rout and villager you help weakens the island's villain before the showdown.
  • Pirates in the streets. Now and then a village the pirates took: fights through the lanes and over the rooftops, prisoners to free, and a pirate captain who fights the way you do.
  • More from every weapon. Two ultimates to choose between, a second crew move that isn't throwing your friend, and alternate air, sprint and heavy attacks you unlock with mastery.

When

As each piece lands. The test builds come every few days, and 1.26 goes out as a stable release once it's all in and played through. Follow along on the roadmap.

Fair winds,
