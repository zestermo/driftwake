1.27: Fortresses and Fruit

Storm the Admiralty's fortress, survive the Scouring, and choose from ten Driftfruits.

1.27 is the biggest release yet. Somewhere off your chain there's now an island the Admiralty doesn't want you to find, the Driftfruits are back as a whole system, and the cities got a lot deeper.

The Admiralty's stronghold

Every world hides one stronghold, off the chain and easy to miss. Beat an Admiralty officer at sea for their orders, catch a rumour at the tavern, or ask the Queen, and it goes on your chart. Then sail in under the Verdict, the great tower you can see from miles off.

  • A raid, not a dungeon crawl. Districts to fight through: the Lower Ward, the Arsenal, the Prison and the Keep, with sealed rooms that fight back in waves, officers hunting you through the halls, and a sewer drain if you'd rather sneak in.
  • Three officers. Lieutenant Kesh in the Casting Hall, where the floor runs molten. Captain Marrow in the prison yard, who throws seastone nets that leave a Driftfruit user powerless, opens the cell block and takes to the catwalks. And Commodore Vane, who fights like a captain and gets worse every phase. Kesh and Marrow each hold a seal to the Keep; take them in either order.
  • Muster flags. Cut down the Admiralty's flag in each district and raise yours. Fall there and you're back up at the flag. Wipe and it's the brig.
  • The Scouring. When the Commodore falls, every ship in the Admiralty hears it. You get two minutes to loot the vault and its legendary weapon, then shells. Get to your ship and sail. If you're famous enough, an Admiral comes ashore to stop you.

Ten Driftfruits

Ember, Storm and Gust are logia: you become the element. Wolf and Bear are zoans. Vine, Iron, Bubble, Clockwork and Echo are paramecia, and each plays its own way. Clockwork rewinds your last three seconds and stops a foe in time. Echo marks enemies through walls and makes your blows land twice. Bubble mostly helps your crew.

  • Five powers each, every one with its own cast: a wind-up, the hit landing on the strike, and effects in that fruit's colours.
  • Two ultimates to choose between, and an awakening at mastery 30.
  • Powers that grow by use. Use one enough and it starts firing by itself at its moment: when you're hit, when you're surrounded, when you dodge or land a kill. Use it more and it fires even off your bar.
  • A full tree per fruit, with mechanics, an alternate power, a bent toward power or flow, and a crew technique for two.
  • Your body changes. Iron turns your arms to polished steel. Vine wraps your limbs. Wolf and Bear give you a half-beast form with the animal's head, mane and claws, and their awakening makes you the full beast: a lumbering bear or a feral wolf.

Cities with more in them

Cellars and smugglers' tunnels under the canal city, catacombs under the capital, houses with stairs to an upper floor, and streets that change at night: lanterns, a night watch, and people out late. Climb the palace's grand stair, earn the Crown's favour and the Republic's, and follow the Queen's own line of work, all the way to a writ for the Admiralty's fortress.

What's next

Driftfruits out in the world: in chests, on enemy captains, and the sea's price for carrying one. The roadmap has the rest.

Every change is in the release notes.
