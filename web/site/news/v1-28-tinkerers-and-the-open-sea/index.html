1.28: Tinkerers and the Open Sea

Build gadgets, cook and fish your way down the chain, pick up a rapier, a staff or a chain sickle, and meet what lives between the islands.

1.28 fills in the life of a pirate between the fights. You can tinker, read, cook and fish now, three new weapons join the rack, two new kinds of island turn up on the chain, and the sea between the islands has places to stop and things you'd rather not meet.

Gadgets and the workbench

A belt of three gadgets on U (O picks the next): smoke and flash pellets, tar and pepper bombs, burst stars, caltrops, a straw decoy, a grappling hook, spring boots, a pocket glider, a climbing spike, a marking spyglass, a diving helmet, an auto-reel, a lockpick and a lantern bomb. Build them at a workbench from blueprints and parts, upgrade them to Mk III, and refill them at any camp. Your ship gets its own: a burst launcher, a paddle boost, a smoke screen and a harpoon cannon.

Reading, cooking and fishing

  • Books. Twenty-nine of them in chests, libraries and shops. Each teaches something: a blueprint, a recipe, a technique, a sea chart, or a piece of a story. The cabin's bookshelf keeps them.
  • Cooking you play. Stir, flip and plate at the galley in first person. Burnt, Fair, Good or Perfect sets how strong the meal is. Every island has its own ingredient and two dishes, and a crew feast feeds everyone aboard.
  • Fishing by island. New catches in every theme's waters, a legendary in each that fights on the line, a fish journal with rewards, spearfishing on a deep dive, and fishing contests in town.

Three new weapons

  • The rapier. A fencer's thrusts and a riposte off every parry, with mirror images and quicksilver.
  • The fighting staff. Held by its middle so both ends strike: overhead blows, launchers, spins and a vault kick, in a whirl of green and gold leaves.
  • The chain sickle. A sickle close in and a weighted chain out to five paces that drags them to you. Wraps, binds and pulls, burning with cold green ghostfire.

Two new islands

The volcanic isle: black sand, lava flows, a forge town with a Master Tinkerer, eruptions that rain lava bombs, and a magma salamander in the caldera. The coral atoll: a ring of islets round a lagoon you can wade, a stilt town over the water, a blue hole to dive, and a giant hermit crab that runs for a new shell when you crack its old one.

The open sea

Now and then something waits on the way to the next island: a floating restaurant with a cook-off, a wandering merchant ship, a warlord's flagship or an Admiralty warship to board and duel. And three leviathans: a colossal squid that grabs your rails, a storm eel that comes with the lightning, and an abyssal angler that only rises at night. Harpoon one and reel it alongside to finish it.
