1.29: Shipshape

Make your sloop a home: a cabin that grows, a pantry for the crew, your own flag and figurehead, and a coat worn over the shoulders.

1.29 is about the ship you live on. The cabin below the quarterdeck has been rebuilt and grows as you upgrade it, the galley gets a pantry the whole crew shares, and there are new ways to make the sloop look like yours. There are new clothes to wear aboard too.

A cabin that grows

The crew's cabin now steps down into the hull, so there's room to stand and move. Tackett sells a new upgrade, Quarters, in four levels: a longer cabin, then stern windows and a chart desk, then a taller deckhead with the captain's corner behind a partition, and at the top a great cabin with a long mess table, a padded bench and a stove against a brick wall. Each level shows from outside too. Rest in the bunk and you're Rested for a while; the finer your quarters, the longer it lasts.

The galley and the pantry

The galley has a pantry now, shared by everyone aboard. Press Stow food to move your ingredients in, and cooking takes from the pantry first, then your bag. The galley and bunks are furnished in detail at every level: hanging pots, a spice rack, jars and a cold box, hammocks and bedding.

Make her yours

  • The wheel. A new, detailed wheel, and every Rudder & helm upgrade shows on it.
  • Your jolly roger. Build a crew flag from a skull, what's crossed behind it and a hat or crown.
  • Paint. Colour the rails and trim, the masts and spars, the deck, and a stripe along the hull.
  • Figureheads. Ten of them, rebuilt and new: a mermaid, a lion, a serpent, an eagle, a ram, a dragon, a shark, a kraken, a crowned skull and a maiden with a lantern.

New clothes

Tick DRAPE in your inventory to wear any coat over your shoulders, sleeves swinging behind you. A new face slot holds masks, kerchiefs, mouth guards and bandages under any hat, and there are arm and foot wraps, parachute pants, a jester's outfit with its bells, and a backpack with a bedroll and a pot that swing as you run. Clothes and armour are sold together now, at one outfitter.

Also in it

Everything from 1.28.1: the coral atoll's reef to dive, a skimmer board to sail built from its parts, and gadget slots (one gadget, two with a belt, four with a bag).

What's next

Bigger ships to work toward: the brig and the galleon. The roadmap has the rest.

Every change is in the release notes.
