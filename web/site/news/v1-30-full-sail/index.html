1.30: Full Sail

A log that always says what's next, new ways to move, bosses that fight back, a raid on the Admiralty, and two new ships.

1.30 comes from a four-captain playtest of 1.29. The crew kept asking what to do next, so the game now tells them. Moving and fighting got deeper, the bosses and the Admiralty stronghold were reworked, and there are two new ships to buy.

Always a next step

The objective box never goes blank. When you follow nothing else it shows the chain's next step: the story, Morrow, a level, then "Sail to" the next island with its bearing and distance, and on an island its elder, its trouble and its beast. The Captain's Log (J) has NEXT, QUESTS, NEWS and DONE tabs. Leads are numbered, marked as yours, a crewmate's or shared, and match the numbers on the map; press T to follow one. Important news pins as a card until you open the log. The capital's lines and the stronghold run are real quests now, and hostile enemies show a small health bar with a level badge.

Move like a pirate

  • Slide. Press C running down a hill to slide, and jump out of it with your speed.
  • Walls. Wall jump, wall kick, wall run, wall cling, spring heels and rebound.
  • Air. Air dash and twin dash, dash strike, a dash straight up, hang time, aerial combos, air control and feather fall.
  • Skill maps. Every tree is one branching map you zoom and pan. Abilities are gold circles, passives blue diamonds, and links between branches dashed coral.
  • Step-up. Walk into a ledge up to about 0.6 m and you climb onto it.

Bosses and fights

Bosses scale for the crew. At each new phase a boss leaps back to the middle of its arena, lands with a shockwave and roars while it can't be hurt, so nobody gets pinned in a corner for two minutes. Crowd one for a few seconds and it roars you off. A big overkill throws a foe flat, backwards or into a wall, where it cracks the stone and slides down, and strong foes can do it to you. The gap between your level and a foe's changes the damage both ways. Pirates and marines have 30% more health, and berserkers, opportunists, duelists and bulwarks each have a tell you can read.

Raid the Admiralty

The stronghold is a raid in acts: breach the gate, take both seals, face Vane, open the vault and escape the Scouring. There's a placed garrison and an alarm, silent takedowns, boss set pieces for Kesh, Marrow and Vane, and objectives a crew splits between them. The vault holds the first Driftfruit. The fortress is dressed in white stone with navy and gold, banners, battlements and warships round the harbour, and the escape is smoke, fire and broadsides. Nessa's and the chandlers now sell smoke, flash, powder charges and the grappling hook.

Bigger ships, a fuller sea

The canal city's shipyard sells the brig, with two masts, a gun deck and a two-room cabin, and the galleon, with three masts, a walkable gun deck, a high stern castle and a library stateroom. Keep every ship you own; paint and figurehead go with you. Pirates sail alongside and trade broadsides, and rival crews arrive in a ship you can see, anchoring off the harbour and calling you out by name. The warlord's flagship and the Admiralty warship are rebuilt as three-masters, and the floating restaurant is twice the size, with a carved fish's head for a bow and a candlelit dining room.

The world

  • The deep reef. The atoll's seabed is dressed, and a deep basin of towering coral, caves, shoals, rays, turtles and reef sharks lies below.
  • Redtide's mountain. Morrow's arena sits in a bowl of red rock rising from the sea.
  • New creatures for the fog and forest islands, and clearer objectives on every island.
  • Brinehollow gets an ironwood grove, a chapel square with three walk-in houses and a new lighthouse.
  • Solid ground. The capital's roofs, the fortress floors and far more of the world are solid.

Also in it

Menus laid out for 1080p, an Unstuck button in the pause menu, a transmog box on every clothing slot, a gentler helm camera, calmer crowds and a chain sickle that moves like a reaper's.

What's next

Life after Brinehollow and the voyage between islands: places with a story and a cast, and a winding route from one to the next. The roadmap has the rest.

Every change is in the release notes.
