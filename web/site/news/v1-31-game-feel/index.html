1.31: Game Feel

A new look, a new way to fight, motion everywhere, and clothes that finally hang like clothes.

1.31 is about how the game feels in your hands and how it looks on screen. Light falls in bold steps with ink round every silhouette, attacks go where you push, the camera frames a fight for you, and bodies move with weight. It also rolls in everything from the 1.30.3 test build: the clothing rework, new gear slots and new music at sea.

A new look

Dithered cel shading: sunlight and moonlight fall in two steps with the PS1's own dither between them, warm in the light and cool in shadow, with brown ink round silhouettes. The sea goes toon with it, white lines riding the crests. Foliage leans and gusts with the weather's wind, and hair and coats stream in a storm. Prefer the old look? Video > Cel shading turns it off.

Fight where you push

  • Omni attacks. Swings go the way you push and snap onto the foe there, and a combo keeps its first direction. There's no crosshair in melee: an amber chevron marks the foe your next swing hits.
  • Lock-on. Middle mouse (R3 on a pad) holds a foe: the camera keeps it framed, techniques and skills aim at it, and a flick switches to the next.
  • A camera for fights. With your weapon out near foes, the camera draws back to fit the fight and leans toward it without ever turning your view (Gameplay > Combat camera).
  • Screen feel. A red edge at low health that pulses harder near death, a blink when you're knocked flat, speed streaks when you fly, a bob at a sprint and a dip on landing (Gameplay > Screen effects).

Motion everywhere

Captains bank into turns, step round on the spot, jolt when a blow lands without staggering them, and shift their weight while they wait. Ropes and grapples work in either hand with one-arm pulls. Every deck job has its own work: pumping, hammering a patch, beating out a fire, stitching a sail, sawing a grapple line and leaning on the capstan. Fire the skimmer in a jump over water and you land on it; lean to steer, Space to jump off. And Roll Out, a third landing skill, rolls you through tall drops unhurt.

Clothes that hang

From 1.30.3: coats, jackets and skirts hang from the shoulders and waist as sewn cloth, with no gaps between panels and nothing clipping your legs. There are new gear slots for Shoulders, Back, Strap and two Trinkets. Pauldrons ride the shoulder, the hakama splits into wide trouser legs, and capes wrap round the back.

New icons

Every skill and item icon is redrawn in one inked, cel-shaded style at a bigger size: 255 skill pictures, 79 items, and the weapons, fish, gadgets and clothes the game draws from their own models. The skill trees show each ability as a picture tile.

Music and sound

Eight calm sea tracks play at random under sail, and storms have their own. The title, the wilds and the harbour town are rewritten in an open piano style. Rooms echo by their size, rain drums on roofs, and far cannons boom across the water.

Also in it

Reviving a crewmate takes F over everything else, crewmates see your glider and your rope swings, hold E to pick gadgets from the item wheel, and on a pad hold B to crouch. Saves keep your bounty, sea chest and arena rank when you load with a weapon in hand, quitting no longer hangs while an island fills with people, and crowded fights run smoother.

What's next

Life after Brinehollow and the voyage between islands: places with a story and a cast, and a winding route from one to the next. The roadmap has the rest.

Every change is in the release notes.
